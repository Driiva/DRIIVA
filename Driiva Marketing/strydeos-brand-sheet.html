07 — Usage Rules
Do / Don't
Non-negotiable. These protect the mark, the system, and the brand's legibility at all sizes and contexts.
✓ Do
Use The Monolith as the sole logo. It is complete and final. No other marks represent StrydeOS.
✗ Don't
Use the old double-chevron stride mark or any A-frame/triangle variant. They are retired. Delete the asset files.
✓ Do
Use #1C54F2 as the primary blue on all CTAs, logo containers, and links. This is the v3.0 electric blue.
✗ Don't
Use the old #1648BC anywhere in production. All assets have migrated to #1C54F2. Run a global find-replace if unsure.
✓ Do
Use DM Serif Display at weight 400 only. Use italic sparingly for hero copy and pull quotes.
✗ Don't
Bold DM Serif Display. It ships at 400 only. Adding bold destroys its character and breaks typographic hierarchy.
✓ Do
Place the mark on #080E1A, #0B2545, or #F2F1EE. The glassmorphic gradient reads on all three backgrounds.
✗ Don't
Place the mark on a solid blue background — the container disappears. Don't add halos, boxes, or rings around it.
✓ Do
Use Teal for Continuity and Purple for Intelligence — as accent colors within those modules only.
✗ Don't
Use Teal or Purple as global brand accents. They are module-exclusive. Breaking this destroys the product system's legibility.
Wordmark Rules

"StrydeOS" is always one word. The "OS" suffix carries electric blue on dark. On light, it takes full primary blue #1C54F2.

Never: "Stryde OS" (space), "strydeos" (all lower), "STRYDEOS" (all caps). Never italicise the wordmark.
